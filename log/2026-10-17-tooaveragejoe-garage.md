A new tower, the Garage (100 scrap), from the user's garage painting. Three mechanics, drawn like the game's other
troops in the painting mechanics' gunmetal and red, each with his tool (a drill, a rivet gun, a wrench), block the road and take a beating (240/360/520 HP, armour 0.3/0.4/0.5, they patch themselves up between
fights) but deal little damage (4/6/8 a hit). Every enemy inside its range moves 25/30/35% slower, flyers too (a boss
half as much), shown by a red field on the ground and a red ring round each slowed enemy. The build menu has six
buttons now, in a hexagon.
